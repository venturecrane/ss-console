# Discipline - the withheld read, the no-fabrication line, no-dollarize, voice

These four rules are the skill. Get them right and the draft is safe and sells the call; get them wrong and it either fabricates (a P0 violation) or gives away the read (kills the conversion).

## 1. The withheld-read boundary (the seam - [ADR 0039](../../../../docs/adr/0039-operator-led-assessment-funnel.md) §1, §4)

The operator drafts the **X-ray**: what is, and where it strains, evidence-bound. The human delivers the **read**: what it means, what matters most, what to do. Hold the line precisely:

| You MAY write (X-ray)                                                                                 | You MUST withhold (the read)                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Dispatch runs through the owner every morning; when he's away it waits."                             | "This owner-dependency is your single biggest risk." ← **verdict**       |
| "Job profitability isn't known until the bookkeeper closes, ~60 days late."                           | "Fix the job-costing first, then scheduling." ← **prioritization**       |
| "Half the techs still text instead of using the field app."                                           | "You should consolidate onto one system and enforce adoption." ← **fix** |
| "There are addressable strains here, and the next conversation is where we prioritize them with you." | Ranking those strains, or naming which to do first. ← **prioritization** |

The withheld read is not stinginess - it is the human's premium judgment, and withholding it from the report is **precisely what makes the prospect book the call.** A draft that delivers the verdict has both broken the seam and removed the reason to book. Convey **solvability** ("this is the kind of thing that's very fixable") in general terms; never convert solvability into a specific prescription.

## 2. The no-fabrication line (P0 - venture law)

Only what the owner gave you. This is the same P0 anti-fabrication law that governs all client-facing content.

- **No invented facts.** No revenue, headcount, named champion, commitment, or timeline the owner did not state.
- **No finding for an un-reached domain.** If the interview never got to pipeline, the pipeline section says _"Not covered in this conversation."_ - it does not get a plausible-sounding finding. Absence is data.
- **No laundered agreement.** If the interviewer proposed a number and the owner merely agreed to be agreeable ("yeah, probably"), that number is **not** the owner's fact - do not record it. (This is the failure the `no-numbers` eval persona is built to catch.)
- **Every finding is anchored.** A claim with no verbatim transcript quote beneath it is cut, not guessed.

## 3. No-dollarize-the-pain (the one genuine carry-over from Outside View)

Give solvability and the shape of the strain. **Never compute or assert its dollar cost.** No "this is costing you ~$40k/yr," no lost-revenue estimate, no ROI, no payback period. The owner does that math against their own numbers - it is theirs to run, and a fabricated figure is a P0 violation. Naming that a strain is real and addressable is the job; pricing its pain is not.

## 4. Voice

- **Reflect the owner's words; quote, don't paraphrase.** The owner should read the draft and feel heard, not assessed.
- **Plain language.** No consulting jargon, no "operational excellence," no "synergies."
- **No em dashes** in shipped copy (venture style law). No AI-polished parallelism.
- **The owner is the hero.** The draft shows their operation respectfully - gaps are normal growth strain, never failings. We are the guide, not the auditor.
- **Lead with their reality, not our capability.** Never reference what SMD would build; the observation→solution translation is the human's at the close.
