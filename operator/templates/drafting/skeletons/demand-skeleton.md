# SKELETON: Policy-Limits Demand Letter (California, Plaintiff PI)

Firm template. Fixed structure and boilerplate; case-specific content is filled from the matter record.

## How to use this skeleton

Fill every `{{FILL: what goes here | source}}` marker from the matter record named in the marker. Do not delete a section because the record is thin. If the record does not establish the fact, mark it and move on.

**Marker legend (applies to every section):**

| Marker                                                        | Meaning                                              | Rule                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `{{FILL: what goes here \| source}}`                          | Drafting content built from the named record source. | Fill from that source. If the source is silent, convert to a `NOT IN RECORD` marker.                                                                 |
| `{{NOT IN RECORD: what was sought, where it was looked for}}` | The record does not establish this fact.             | Leave the marker in place in the draft. Never supply a plausible substitute. A demand that invents a fact is worse than a demand with a visible gap. |
| `{{ATTORNEY: decision reserved}}`                             | Legal judgment or settlement authority.              | Do not resolve. Present the options and the record bearing on them, then stop.                                                                       |

**Invention rule.** Every figure, date, diagnosis, provider name, and quoted statement in the finished letter must trace to a document in the matter record. Nothing is estimated, rounded up, smoothed, or inferred from what is typical for this kind of case.

**Voice.** Firm boilerplate here is deliberately generic. Register, salutation habits, sign-off, and sentence rhythm come from the firm voice layer, not from this file.

## Statutory compliance checklist (pre-suit demands)

<!-- GUIDANCE: Code of Civil Procedure sections 999 through 999.5 govern time-limited demands made before suit or arbitration on auto, motor vehicle, homeowner, and commercial premises liability policies for property damage, bodily injury, or wrongful death claims. If this demand is not within that scope, or is sent after suit is filed, the section 999 labeling and timing mechanics below do not apply and should be removed rather than adapted. Confirm scope before drafting. -->

Confirm each item before the letter goes out. If any item cannot be satisfied from the record, flag it rather than drafting around it.

- [ ] In writing and labeled as a time-limited demand, or expressly referencing Code of Civil Procedure section 999.
- [ ] Clear and unequivocal offer to settle all claims within the policy limits, inclusive of satisfaction of all liens.
- [ ] Provides for a complete release of the insured.
- [ ] States the date and location of the loss.
- [ ] States the claim number, if known.
- [ ] Describes all known injuries sustained by the claimant.
- [ ] Encloses reasonable proof sufficient to support the claim (records, bills, or both).
- [ ] Acceptance period of not fewer than 30 days from transmission by email, facsimile, or certified mail; not fewer than 33 days if sent by ordinary mail.
- [ ] Transmission method and date recorded in the matter file.

## Pre-transmission review points

Not part of the letter. Confirm before it goes out.

1. Every figure in section IV traces to a bill or lien in the file, and the billed and paid columns are not mixed in the total.
2. No diagnosis is stated as established where the record has it as an impression or a rule-out.
3. No future care or future wage loss is asserted without a written provider or vocational opinion.
4. Every `NOT IN RECORD` marker has been resolved by locating the document or accepted as a visible gap by the attorney. None reach the carrier.
5. Every `ATTORNEY` marker has been resolved by the attorney.
6. The section 999 checklist at the top is fully satisfied, including the acceptance period matching the transmission method.
7. The enclosure list matches what is physically attached.
