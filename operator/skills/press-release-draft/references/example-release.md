# A worked release (synthetic firm, Fresno office)

Synthetic firm: Example & Partners LLP, Fresno. Its registers carry a result
entry for "$1.2 million" with its basis and harm, and the results disclaimer
shown. The administrator's email gave these facts and said the partners
approved them: a $1.2 million settlement; a commercial truck driver ran a red
light; the harm was a fractured spine; the client consented to a release that
names no one; the media contact is the office manager at
media@example.com.

```
--- DRAFT press ---
Example & Partners LLP resolves truck collision claim for $1.2 million

Fresno, California, November 12, 2026. Example & Partners LLP announced today that it resolved a claim arising from a collision with a commercial truck for $1.2 million.

The result portrayed was dependent on the facts of that case, and results will differ if based on different facts.

The claim arose after a commercial truck driver ran a red light. The person the firm represented suffered a fractured spine.

About Example & Partners LLP
Example & Partners LLP is a personal injury law firm in Fresno, California.

Media contact: the office manager, media@example.com

###
--- END DRAFT ---
```

Why it passes: every fact is from the approved email; the figure matches the
registers' result entry, and the results disclaimer sits right after it; the
basis and the harm are stated; no superlative, quote, client name or
identifying detail; the dateline is in ordinary case; the firm's name and
office city appear. The cover still asks the three questions (consent,
publicity terms and pending matters, the approving lawyer and the wire).
