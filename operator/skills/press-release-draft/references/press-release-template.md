# Press release template

Plain text. No exclamation marks, no ALL-CAPS words (the dateline city is
written in ordinary case), no em dashes, no markdown.

```
<Headline: what happened, plainly, under 100 characters>

<Office city>, California, <Month D, YYYY>. <Firm name> <what happened, from the approved facts, one or two sentences>.

<Results disclaimer, word for word, when the release states a result>

<Second paragraph: the approved facts that explain the result: the basis of liability and the nature of the harm, as the email states them. California requires the facts that justify a result, or the disclaimer above.>

<Approved quote, exact words, with the speaker as the roster names them. Omit when none was given.>

About <firm name>
<firm name> is a personal injury law firm in <office city>, California. <one or two more sentences the firm approved, or none>.

Media contact: <name, email or phone as the email gives them>

###
```

Notes:

- The headline may carry a figure only when the paragraph right after the
  first one carries the results disclaimer.
- A superlative or comparison needs its source named in the same sentence.
- "###" is the press-release end mark, not a heading.
