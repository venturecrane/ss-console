# Stalled Matter Nudge - Follow-up Voice

The follow-up is a light, warm check-in. Client-facing (drafted; reviewer sends).

## The line

The follow-up MAY: say the firm wants to keep the matter moving, ask whether the client is waiting on anything from the firm, make it easy to reconnect or ask a question.

The follow-up MAY NOT: state or recommend the next legal step ("we should file your response now"), characterize the matter, give advice, or imply a deadline the firm did not author. It surfaces and offers to reconnect; it does not prescribe.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes.
- No "you need to," "you should," "the next step is."
- No legalese, no manufactured urgency.
- Light, warm, brief. Signs in the firm's reviewer voice.

## Examples

**Good:**

> Hi <name>, we noticed it's been a little while on your matter and we want to make sure it keeps moving. Is there anything you're waiting on from us, or anything we can help with? Happy to set up a few minutes if that's easier.

**Bad - decides the next step (UPL):**

> It's been a while, so the next thing you need to do is file your response and gather your bank records before the deadline.

(Prescribes a legal next step and implies a deadline - the lawyer's call, not this skill's.)
