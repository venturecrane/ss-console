What to know about California's rules for e-bike riders

Electric bicycles have become a common sight on Fresno streets. California sorts them into three classes, and the class decides where a rider may go and who may ride.

How the three classes differ

Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes stop helping the rider at 20 miles per hour. Class 3 e-bikes can assist up to 28 miles per hour, and riders must be at least 16 and wear a helmet. Local rules can limit where each class may ride, so it is worth checking the city's own rules for bike paths and trails.

What happens after a collision

In general, anyone involved in a collision should check on the people involved, call for help when someone is hurt, exchange information, and write down what they remember while it is fresh. Deadlines for a claim depend on the facts, there are exceptions, and claims involving a public agency can have a much shorter notice period, so the dates in any one situation are a question for a lawyer.

Where to read the rules

The California Vehicle Code sections on electric bicycles and the DMV's guidance are the primary sources.
