# Industry Packs

This document says, for each industry SMD has mapped, what the Operator takes on in that kind of business and the line it never crosses. A pack is a head start shaped around one seat in that industry, which SMD configures with the client. The work and the line below are the starting point to react to and shape together, not the only way it can run.

## The rule in every industry

- The Operator does the connective work and brings the judgment to the people who hold it.
- Licensed and professional judgment stays with the client's licensed people. Those never move to the Operator. That line is written into every seat's limits.
- It never moves money.
- Every action it takes is logged where the client can see it.
- Where accuracy depends on reading the client's documents and matching its rules, SMD validates it on the client's real work first. Until accuracy is proven, the Operator surfaces what it found and asks, rather than acting on its own. It earns more autonomy as it proves accurate.
- The configuration, the logs and the off switch stay with the client.

## Law firm

- What it takes on: for a personal injury practice, it carries a case from records to settlement. It reads medical records into cited chronologies, builds the billing picture, writes first drafts for the attorneys, and tracks settled cases to payout.
- The line: the lawyer's work stays the lawyer's. The judgment stays with the attorneys: what a case is worth, the strategy, the call on a matter, and what gets signed. The firm sets the lines it works within, such as anything bound for opposing counsel or the court. Trust balances are read, never moved.

## Accounting

- What it takes on: it works inside the firm's practice-management platform and carries an engagement from onboarding to filed, so nothing stalls on a missing document or a deadline.
- The line: it coordinates the engagement. It never gives tax advice and never decides what gets filed. It never takes a tax position, never reads a treatment or a deduction off a document, and never decides whether, when or on which form a return must be filed. It tracks the dates the firm entered and surfaces standard due dates as they approach.
- Taxpayer information: the Operator works inside the firm's tax-return disclosure consents under section 7216 of the tax code. The firm holds the consents, and the seat's limits enforce them. SMD works through the firm's section 7216 posture with it at setup, including any client consent its configuration requires.

## Dental

- What it takes on: the written follow-up work around the front desk, by text and email: recare, treatment-plan follow-up, unpaid claims and patient balances.
- The line: it gives no clinical advice and never promises what insurance will pay. It never diagnoses, recommends or frames a treatment, judges urgency or upsells. Benefits go to the practice's team, never as a coverage number quoted to a patient. Anything that could be a dental emergency goes to a person, and the patient is told to call the office, or 911 for a severe one. SMD signs a business associate agreement before it touches a record.

## Home services

- What it takes on: the office work, in writing: scheduling, arrival windows, on-the-way updates, estimate and invoice follow-up, memberships and recall.
- The line: it never diagnoses and never quotes a price. The tech diagnoses; the company sets the price. It never speaks to code, permits or what a warranty covers; those go to a person. For a life-safety signal, such as a gas smell, a carbon-monoxide alarm, fire or electrical arcing, it tells the customer to leave and call 911 and the gas utility from a safe distance.

## Insurance

- What it takes on: it works inside the agency management system and carries the service and renewal desk, so nothing slips.
- The line: it services the book. It never advises on coverage and never binds. Producers advise, quote and bind; licensed staff make the changes that need a license; the carrier decides the claim. A certificate reflects only the coverage already on the policy. It never binds, changes, cancels or reinstates a policy, and never tells a client a loss is covered.

## Marketing agency

- What it takes on: the account coordination work, in writing: onboarding, status, the asset chase, sign-off, deadlines, invoices and reports.
- The line: it delivers what the agency authored and never reframes a number, a result or a claim. It works each of the agency's clients in its own walled-off context, with separate data and memory, and nothing crosses between them. It never commits scope, a deliverable, a date or a price on its own.

## Medical spa

- What it takes on: the patient-journey work, in writing: leads, consults, the good-faith-exam gate, the provider's pre- and post-care, memberships and reactivation.
- The line: it gives no medical advice and never clears anyone for treatment. It enforces the medical director's rule that the good-faith exam is on record before a treatment, and never substitutes for the exam. Anything that could be an adverse reaction goes to the provider through a channel the spa monitors in real time, and the client is directed to the spa's urgent contact, or to 911 for severe symptoms. SMD signs the agreement that health information requires before it touches a record.

## Mortgage

- What it takes on: it works inside the loan origination system and carries a loan from application to funding, so nothing slips.
- The line: it coordinates and never decides. The loan officer advises on products, rates and the lock; the underwriter approves, denies and conditions. Every borrower message goes to a person. It never transmits, confirms or changes wire instructions; a question about where to wire funds goes to the lender's verified process.

## Property management

- What it takes on: it works inside the property-management platform and carries the resident and owner lifecycle, so no one waits on the coordinator.
- The line: it gives every prospect the same published requirements and the same path, and never decides who qualifies. It never infers, asks about or acts on a protected class, and routes steering questions to a person. A reasonable-accommodation request goes to a person at once. It never touches the rent.

## Registered investment advisers

- What it takes on: it works inside the advisor CRM and carries the client-service-associate work, so accounts stay in order.
- The line: it never advises and never moves money. It has no transactional authority anywhere. It never captures or alters payee or banking details, and any instruction to move money or change banking or contact details goes to the firm's verified process. Client communications are written back into the firm's system of record, so its records retention stays inside the firm.

## Title and escrow

- What it takes on: it works inside the title production system and carries a closing from the open order to recorded, so nothing slips.
- The line: it never opines on title and never touches the money. The examiner owns the title opinion; the escrow officer owns the figures and the funds. Any message about wire instructions, bank details or where to send funds goes to the company's verified process. It never moves or disburses escrow funds.

## Veterinary

- What it takes on: the front-desk work, in writing: booking, wellness recall, refill relay, released results, estimates and boarding.
- The line: it never plays doctor and never triages. It never diagnoses, interprets a result, authorizes a refill or sets a dose, and it flags a controlled substance for the doctor. It never rates how serious a symptom is: a message that could be urgent goes to a person fast, and the client is told to call the clinic or the after-hours emergency number.

## Real estate

- What it takes on: a starting preset for open-house visitor capture, run on a seat SMD pays for. A real estate seat is shaped with the client the same way as every other pack.
- The line: the same rule as every industry, above.
