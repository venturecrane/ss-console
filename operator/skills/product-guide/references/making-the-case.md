# Making the Case

This document is for someone inside a business who wants to bring the Operator to the people who decide. It covers who usually decides in an owner-led business, the questions decision makers ask and where this library answers each one, how data is kept in plain words, and what to show them. Every answer names the document and section it comes from, so the decision makers can read the source themselves. It says nothing about any particular business: what SMD would set up for a given business is a conversation with SMD Services at team@smd.services.

## Who decides in an owner-led business

- **The owner or principal signs.** Only the client's principal can start, pay for or cancel the service, and only the principal decides how far each routine goes within the agreed ceiling. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Who does what".)
- **How the business works today is written down first.** Onboarding includes a workshop on how the business works today; the client decides, and SMD records it. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases".)
- **Whoever runs the business's systems grants access.** The client's administrator authorizes the Operator into each real system with SMD, and each one is checked with a live read. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases" and "Who does what".)
- **A professional's judgment stays with the professional.** Licensed and professional judgment stays with the client's licensed people, and that line is written into the Operator's limits. (From [industry-packs.md](industry-packs.md), "The rule in every industry".)

## What could go wrong, and what stops it

- **Nothing runs that was not written down.** A kind of action with no limit written is refused, and a missing or garbled setting gives no access. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Rules that hold everywhere".)
- **It cannot give itself more authority.** Its configuration is read-only to it, and only the client can widen a limit, up to the agreed ceiling. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Rules that hold everywhere".)
- **Agreements and anything it cannot undo wait for a person.** Commitments and destructive actions need a person's approval at the time, even where a routine otherwise acts on its own. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Ceilings from least to most restrictive".)
- **Untrusted content cannot drive it.** On a turn where it read an email from an unknown sender, a web page or a document, it may read and draft, but sends, commitments and deletions are refused. The only exceptions are a few sends whose recipients that content cannot change, and each is marked in the audit record. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Rules that hold everywhere".)
- **It never moves money, and licensed judgment never moves to it.** (From [industry-packs.md](industry-packs.md), "The rule in every industry".)
- **AI output can be wrong, so review is the normal path.** The client's people review its work before relying on it. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "AI output and the client's review".)
- **A stuck or costly job stops instead of guessing.** Each queued job checks its cost limits before every paid step, is reviewed against its sources, and is recorded as delivered only with proof. (From [jobs.md](jobs.md), "From request to delivery".)

## Who controls it, and how far it goes

- **The client writes the limits.** For every kind of action, the client decides whether the Operator acts on its own, asks first, prepares a draft for a person, or may not do it at all. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "What the client controls".)
- **New routines start careful.** SMD's practice is to start a new routine at "Prepares it for you", drafts for a person, and move it up only as the client chooses, never past the agreed ceiling. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Rules that hold everywhere".)
- **The client sees and directs it.** The Client portal shows the Operator's role, duties, access and the people it works with, its skills and what starts each one, and its activity, including who changed what. (From [client-controls.md](client-controls.md), "Direct" and "Account".)
- **Changes are asked for, and tracked.** Anyone with a portal role can file a change request, and it is tracked until it is acknowledged, resolved or declined. (From [client-controls.md](client-controls.md), "Request a change".)

## What the people affected see

- **Staff.** There is one Operator per client, and staff email it like a colleague, with nothing new to log in to. A message in a staff member's own name goes out only after that person approves it by replying. (From [how-the-operator-works.md](how-the-operator-works.md), "Before and after: what changes for the business", and [connections-and-channels.md](connections-and-channels.md), "Email channels".)
- **Customers and clients.** They hear from the business on the channels it chooses. For each kind of message, the business decides whether the draft goes to someone on its team or goes out on its own. (From [connections-and-channels.md](connections-and-channels.md), "Other channels", and [how-the-operator-works.md](how-the-operator-works.md), "The permission model: the client writes the limits".)
- **There is a record to check.** Every action is logged where the client can see it, and the client's principal and compliance role can download the audit record for a reference from the Client portal. (From [industry-packs.md](industry-packs.md), "The rule in every industry", and [client-controls.md](client-controls.md), "Account".)

## How a pilot is shaped and measured

The shape and terms of any pilot are set with SMD Services at team@smd.services. What the library says about how the Operator is set up and proven:

- **It is shaped around the business first.** A working session on how the business runs comes before anything is built, and the client sets the limit for each kind of action. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases".)
- **It is rehearsed before it touches real work.** SMD rehearses on practice data it owns, including hostile cases, and moves on only when no limit is broken. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases".)
- **It comes on one area at a time.** Each area of work is turned on only when the client confirms it on its own work. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases".)
- **It earns autonomy.** Where accuracy depends on reading the client's documents and matching its rules, SMD validates it on the client's real work first, and until accuracy is proven the Operator surfaces what it found and asks. (From [industry-packs.md](industry-packs.md), "The rule in every industry".)
- **New kinds of work start as a trial.** SMD may do the first instances of a new kind of work as a trial, measure them, and then propose terms in writing. (From [how-the-operator-works.md](how-the-operator-works.md), "What managed means".)
- **What it did is on the record.** The audit record holds one entry for every tool call, and the client's principal and compliance role can download it from the Client portal by reference, so the work is judged against what actually happened. (From [client-controls.md](client-controls.md), "Account", and [isolation-and-data-custody.md](isolation-and-data-custody.md), "The audit record".)
- **Billing starts only after testing.** Billing starts when SMD states in writing that testing is complete and the client's principal starts the subscription. Nobody at SMD can start it. (From [managed-service.md](managed-service.md), "Onboarding phases".)

## How it is paused or stopped

- **Pause.** The client can pause the Operator in the Client portal, with a reason. While paused, every tool call is refused, and queued jobs wait and resume when the pause lifts. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "What the client controls", [client-controls.md](client-controls.md), "Direct", and [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Pause, stop and safety".)
- **Cut access.** The client can also cut the Operator's access in its own systems. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "What the client controls".)
- **It stops itself.** A safety breaker stops the seat on repeated failures or refusals, and it stays stopped until a person clears it. (From [permission-model.md](permission-model.md), "Pause, stop and safety".)
- **End the service.** The client can cancel in the Client portal or by letter. On exit the client receives its audit record and the Operator's memory in exportable form, and SMD destroys the client's machine and storage and confirms it in writing. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "Ending the service, return and deletion".)

## Where the data lives, in plain words

- **Its own machine.** Each client's Operator runs on its own dedicated machine with its own storage and credentials, and no other client shares them. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "How client data is used, and what it is never used for".)
- **The business's systems stay the systems of record.** SMD does not warehouse copies of the client's files; the Operator reads what a task needs through connections the client has granted. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "Who owns the data".)
- **Credentials stay on the client's machine.** Connection credentials never go into a shared store, and the credentials that can send are held apart from the part that does the work. (From [isolation-and-data-custody.md](isolation-and-data-custody.md), "Credential custody".)
- **Only AI agents handle client files.** Client files are handled only by the client's own Operator and by SMD's agents in a workspace sealed to that client. No person at SMD reads client files. SMD's console shows governance records, never business content. (From [isolation-and-data-custody.md](isolation-and-data-custody.md), "What SMD can see beyond the console".)
- **Requests are watched.** So that every request gets an answer, SMD's operations mailbox receives a notice of each request with the requester's words and the Operator's reply. (From [isolation-and-data-custody.md](isolation-and-data-custody.md), "What SMD can see beyond the console".)
- **No training, no mixing.** Client data is never used to train or fine-tune a model, and one client's data never flows to another client's Operator. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "How client data is used, and what it is never used for".)
- **The client owns it.** The client owns its data and the memory built for it, and can ask for an export of the memory and the audit record during the term. (From [what-smd-commits-to.md](what-smd-commits-to.md), "Who owns the data".)
- **The record cannot be rewritten.** The audit record is chained and verified every day, and the Operator cannot change it. (From [isolation-and-data-custody.md](isolation-and-data-custody.md), "The audit record".)
