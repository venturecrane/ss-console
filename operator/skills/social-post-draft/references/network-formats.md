# Network formats

All three: plain text, no markdown, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no
ALL-CAPS words, the firm's name and office city in the body (unless the
registers say the profile carries them), one idea per post.

## Facebook

- 40 to 80 words. The first sentence carries the point; Facebook folds longer
  text behind "See more".
- A link to the firm's own post is fine, on its own line at the end.
- Hashtags: none or one or two at the end.
- Image line: the published post's featured image, or a described safe scene.

## Instagram

- 60 to 125 words for the caption; the first line is what shows in the feed.
- **No link in the caption.** Links in Instagram captions do not work. Write
  "The full post is on our website." and nothing more about the link.
- Three to five plain hashtags at the end, lowercase, no claims (for example
  #fresno #roadsafety #california). Never a hashtag that claims a result or a
  rank.
- Instagram needs an image: the image line is required.

## X

- At most 280 characters including spaces. A link counts as 23 characters
  whatever its length, so a post with a link has 257 characters for its text.
- One link at most, to the firm's own post. One hashtag at most.
- The firm name and city still go in the text; keep the sentence short.

## Images on social

The same rules as the blog: no injury, no accident scene, no hospital scene, no
person presented as a client, no check or cash. A published blog post's
featured image can be reused. Otherwise describe a safe scene in one line:
"Image: a crosswalk at dusk with the walk signal lit, no people's faces".
