# New Matter Intake - Acknowledgment Voice

The acknowledgment is the only client-facing text this skill produces, and it is where the UPL line is most easily crossed. The voice is warm, plainspoken, and **non-committal about the legal matter**. A non-lawyer reviewer must be able to send it as-is.

## The line

The acknowledgment MAY: confirm the inquiry arrived, thank them for reaching out, say a member of the firm will follow up, name a next step **the firm authored** (e.g., "we'll be in touch to find a time to talk" - only if the firm authored that flow).

The acknowledgment MAY NOT: say or imply the firm will represent them ("we can take this on," "you're in good hands"), characterize the matter ("this sounds like a strong claim," "you have a case"), give any legal information ("you have two years to file"), promise a specific time the firm did not author ("a lawyer will call you within 24 hours"), or refer them elsewhere unless the firm authored a referral.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes.
- No "I hope this email finds you well," "just following up," "touching base."
- No legalese, no "pursuant to," no "we will zealously."
- Active voice, short sentences, plainspoken.
- Signs in the firm's reviewer voice (the human sends under their own identity) - not as a named attorney unless the firm authored that.
- If the agent can't write an acknowledgment that holds the line, mark it `LOW` and write a one-line plan instead of prose.

## Examples

**Good - clean intake:**

> Thank you for reaching out to the firm. We've received the details about your situation, and someone from our team will follow up to talk it through with you. If anything changes in the meantime, just reply here.

**Good - conflict-hold, receipt only:**

> Thank you for reaching out to the firm. We've received your message and someone from our team will be in touch. We appreciate your patience.

**Bad - adopts merits (UPL):**

> Thanks for the details. This sounds like a strong breach-of-contract case and we'd be glad to represent you. Expect a call within 24 hours.

(Three violations: characterizes merits, promises representation, invents a timeframe.)

**Bad - gives legal information (UPL):**

> Since your accident was in March, you still have time to file, but don't wait too long.

(States a deadline - legal judgment the firm has not made.)
