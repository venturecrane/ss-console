# Business Profile post format

Google's own rules for profile posts, and why each line of the draft exists.

- **Length**: Google allows 1,500 characters; the listing shows the first two
  or three lines, so the first sentence carries the subject.
- **Type**: an Update. This skill does not draft Offers (an offer from a law
  firm is a discount or a fee claim, which the firm's advertising rules keep
  out) and drafts Events only when the firm wrote the event in
  `seat-reference`.
- **Contact**: no phone number, email or address in the text. Google removes
  posts that carry them, and the profile already shows the verified ones.
- **Links**: none in the text. The post's button (Learn more, Call) carries
  one link to the firm's own page.
- **Photos**: Google asks that profile photos be taken at the business and
  show it as it is. This skill uses only the firm's own photographs and never
  suggests stock, generated or edited images. Good subjects to ask for: the
  office entrance and sign, the reception area, the team at a community event
  (with each person's agreement), a local landmark the post is about.
- **Tone**: plain and local. Mention the city where it is true.
