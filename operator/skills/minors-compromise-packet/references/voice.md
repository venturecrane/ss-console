# Minor's Compromise Packet - Voice

Derives from `operator/verticals/law-firm/addons/pi/references/_shared-chase-voice.md`.

This skill's only outbound draft is the **lien payoff chase**. The packet itself
(MC-350 / MC-351 and any fund-handling forms) is an internal, staged artifact filled
from authored figures; it carries no voice beyond the forms' own fields. Neither the
chase nor the packet is ever sent or filed without a human.

## The lien payoff chase (to a lienholder - a professional, not a client)

Professional and businesslike. It names the one outstanding thing (a current payoff
figure for a specific patient/claimant), notes why timing matters at a plain level
(the settlement needs the current figure to move to court approval), and gives the one
next step (send the current payoff, or the authorization to release it).

The chase MAY: state which payoff figure is outstanding and for whom; note that a
current figure is needed for the settlement to proceed to approval; point to the one
action (send the payoff, confirm the amount, or ask for a fresh authorization); offer
to resend the authorization.

The chase MAY NOT: estimate, reduce, dispute, or negotiate the payoff; explain or
characterize any legal term or consequence; imply a figure is agreed or a step is done
that is not observed; state the settlement amount or the minor's net; pressure or
invent urgency.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes.
- No "just circling back," "just following up," "touching base."
- No legalese ("execute," "heretofore," "per our correspondence").
- Short and professional to the lienholder. One clear next step.
- Never assert a payoff figure, a payment, a court approval, or a filing that is not an
  observed fact.
- Never states the minor's net, the settlement gross, or the fee to an outside party.

## Good / bad

**Good - lien payoff chase to a lienholder:**

> Hi <name>, we are preparing the settlement approval for <claimant> and need a current
> payoff figure on your lien (account <ref>) to complete the disclosure. Could you send
> the current amount, or let us know if you need a fresh authorization to release it?
> Happy to resend it. Thank you.

**Bad - estimates / negotiates the payoff (out of bounds):**

> Your lien is probably around $8,000 by now; we will plan on paying $6,000 to close it
> out, so consider it settled unless you object this week.

(Estimates a figure, negotiates it, and asserts a resolution that is not observed.)

**Bad - leaks the minor's figures / characterizes the fairness:**

> The minor is netting $42,000 after our reasonable one-third fee, so your lien is a
> small piece and should be easy to release.

(States the net and the fee, characterizes the fee as reasonable, and pressures.)
