# The reply library

Every reply is built from these sentences. Positive replies may vary the
wording within the same meaning; negative and mixed replies use the sentences
as written, filling only the bracketed parts from the firm's registers.

The negative-review sentences follow the forms the ethics opinions describe as
safe: ABA Formal Opinion 496 (2021), and in California the Bar Association of
San Francisco Opinion 2014-1 and the Los Angeles County Bar Association Opinion 525. None of them describes, confirms or denies a representation.

## Positive (four or five stars)

Pick one opening and, optionally, one closing.

- Opening: "Thank you for taking the time to leave this review."
- Opening: "Thank you for the kind words."
- Opening: "We appreciate you sharing this."
- Closing: "We are glad to be part of the <office city> community."
- Closing: "Thank you again."

Never add what the review praised ("thank you for noticing how responsive we
were" repeats the review), never mention a matter, never invite a referral.

## Empty review (stars, no words)

- "Thank you for the rating."

## Negative or mixed (one to three stars, or any complaint)

Use (a), then (b). Use (b) alone when the offline contact is not authored.

- (a) "We are sorry to hear you are unhappy, and we would welcome the chance
  to talk. Please contact <offline contact name> at <offline contact phone or
  email> so that we can discuss your concerns."
- (b) "Professional obligations do not allow us to respond as we would
  wish."

For a mixed review, open with "Thank you for taking the time to leave this
review." before (a).

## Only when the administrator asks, and only as a question in the cover

- "We respectfully disagree with this description, but we are not at liberty
  to discuss confidential matters." This sentence implies the reviewer was a
  client, so it is offered only when the reviewer has already said so
  publicly and the administrator asks for a response that disagrees. Never put
  it in the draft on your own.
- "Our records show we have not represented this person." Only the firm can
  check this and only a person at the firm can decide to say it. Never put it
  in the draft on your own.

## Never, in any reply

"your case", "your claim", "your matter", "your accident", "your injury",
"your settlement", "we represented", "we handled", "when you hired us",
"thank you for trusting us with", "as your attorneys", "free consultation",
"call us today", any link, any phone number other than the authored offline
contact, "fake", "liar", "lying", "defamatory", "legal action", "we will
report".
