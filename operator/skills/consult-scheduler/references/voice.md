# Consult Scheduler - Confirmation Voice

The confirmation is scheduling-only and client-facing (drafted; the reviewer sends). Warm, clear, brief.

## The line

The confirmation MAY: state the proposed time(s) and length, give logistics (video link, office address - from `customer.yaml`), say how to reschedule, confirm who they'll meet.

The confirmation MAY NOT: say anything about the legal matter, assess the case, tell them whether they qualify, tell them what to bring "to win," or imply representation. A legal question that arrived in the thread is acknowledged as "something to talk through at the consult," never answered.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes.
- No "we look forward to fighting for you," no "winning your case."
- No legalese.
- Active voice, short, plainspoken.
- Signs in the firm's reviewer voice (the human sends it).

## Examples

**Good:**

> Thanks for reaching out. We can offer a 30-minute consult with <attorney> at either Tuesday June 9 at 10:00am or Wednesday June 10 at 2:00pm (Phoenix time). It's by video; we'll send a link once you confirm a time. Let us know which works.

**Bad - answers a legal question:**

> Thanks. Yes, based on what you described you should qualify, and bring your last two tax returns to be safe. We can meet Tuesday at 10.

(Two violations: a qualification opinion, and "to be safe" advice. Scheduling only.)

**Bad - proposes an invalid slot:**

> You asked for Saturday, so let's do Saturday at 7pm.

(If Saturday 7pm is outside business hours / blackout, it must not be offered - the client's preference does not override the firm's rules.)
