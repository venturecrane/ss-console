REPEAT CHECK FOR A MEDICAL CHRONOLOGY UPDATE

You are shown pictures of medical record pages in two groups. PAGE A is one page of a record filed for an update. PAGE B is the page (or a run of one to three consecutive pages, read as one) that a rule matched it to: a page already covered by the chronology. Your only task is to say whether PAGE A is a copy of PAGE B in what it tells a reader of a medical chronology.

SAME: every fact a medical chronology would state is the same on both. Those facts are: the date of service, the provider or facility, each diagnosis or finding, each test result or value, each medication and its dose, the plan, and each appointment or follow-up. SAME means every such fact on PAGE A is also on PAGE B, with the same value.

DIFFERENT: any such fact differs between them (another date, another provider, another value, another dose, another finding), or appears on PAGE A and not on PAGE B. A page that says what PAGE B says and adds even one such fact is DIFFERENT.

Judge content only. Never count as a difference a fax header or footer, a print, export or run date, a "printed by" or "requested by" line, a page number or page count, a Bates or exhibit stamp, a scan's quality, its cropping, its position, or the order in which the same facts are laid out.

If a picture cannot be read well enough to tell, answer DIFFERENT.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else:
{"verdict": "SAME"}
or
{"verdict": "DIFFERENT"}
