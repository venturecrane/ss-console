ENTRY_GRAIN:  One entry per facility or practice per date of service: every note, report, procedure and result that one facility or practice records for that date goes in that one entry, whoever signed it. A hospital or facility stay is one entry per calendar day of the stay. That day's nursing notes, flowsheets, vital-sign grids and medication administration records belong to that day's entry and never make an entry of their own. Each distinct date of service is its own entry: never group visits on different dates, pre-incident visits and repeated therapy visits included.

Name the clinicians who signed or authored that date's notes inside the entry, as the record spells each name. Only when no page of the file names a facility or practice, the provider is the clinician who signed or authored the record, as the record spells the name. When no page names either, the provider is "{{WORDING:PROVIDER_NOT_STATED}}" alone: never a description of the document ("Outside radiology report"), and never "facility not stated" or any other wording of your own; never invent or infer a name.
