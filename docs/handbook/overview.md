SMD Services is a solutions consulting venture under SMDurgan, LLC. We help growing,
owner-led businesses figure out what needs to change operationally and build the right
solution together. It is not a SaaS product. It is a services business, and the delivery
team is AI-native, which is what lets it work faster and at lower cost than a traditional
consultancy.

The venture is run by a single Captain directing a fleet of AI agents. The objective is
to launch and reach profitability.

## Three front doors

The venture sells through three connected funnels:

- **Scope-based consulting.** Bounded engagements priced per project. We assess where a
  business is trying to go, find what is in the way, and build the solution: process
  design, custom internal tools, systems integration, operational visibility,
  vendor selection, or AI and automation. Every engagement runs assessment, solution
  design, implementation, training, and handoff. Scope sets how heavy each phase is, not
  whether it happens.
- **The Operator.** A productized monthly retainer. A configurable AI operator that runs
  on a client firm's expertise and handles the coordinator-grade throughput a growing
  business needs but cannot justify hiring for. See [The Operator Thesis](/admin/playbook/operator-thesis).
- **The Hosted Agent.** A self-serve monthly subscription for an always-on personal
  Hermes agent, run and safety-gated on the same substrate as the Operator. The only
  SKU with published pricing and self-serve checkout; provisioning stays concierge
  behind the storefront (ADR 0067, Decision #51).

## Who it serves

Established, owner-led businesses with real operational load and the ability to pay for a
solution. There is no revenue-band gate; qualification happens in conversation, not by
filtering on a guessed revenue figure. The shape of the buyer is a business too big for
one person to hold and too small to justify a COO. The buyer is the owner, who writes the
check.

The default geography is Phoenix metro, in-person for the first engagements, and
remote-capable after the model is proven.

## The thesis in one line

The Operator competes with a hire, not with software. The comparison is not "is this
better than Salesforce" but "is this better than bringing on a full-time coordinator." It
wins on memory that compounds, configurable autonomy, no context-switching cost, and
enterprise discipline at an SMB price. The moat is the whole stack of harness, guide, and
memory, never a single feature.

## How the venture runs

The Captain decides; the fleet executes. The Captain owns scope, pricing, client
acceptance, escalation resolution, go/kill calls, and platform direction. AI agents handle
throughput: client communication within authored autonomy ceilings, analysis and
deliverable drafting, fleet health monitoring, and platform
maintenance through pull requests. A newcomer stepping into this system inherits a running
operation, not a blank slate.
