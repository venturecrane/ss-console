# 1. What the Operator Is

## The picture

> **Visual V1: The gap and the worker that fills it.** Brief in [Visual briefs](05-visual-briefs.md).

## In one paragraph

Every business has a layer of work that falls between its people and its software: the handoff, the follow-up, the reading nobody has time for. Today a person holds it together by hand. The Operator is a worker we build for one business and run for it. It works inside the systems the business already uses, does only what the business has authorized, keeps a tamper-evident record of everything it does, and keeps what the firm's people teach it, so a correction holds the next time.

## Five things to remember

|     | The idea                                        | Say it like this                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **It competes with a hire, not with software.** | "Think of it as a staff member who happens to be software." Price conversations compare to a salary, never to a subscription.      |
| 2   | **It is built for one business.**               | "We build it around how you already work. You teach it, the way you'd train a new hire."                                           |
| 3   | **The client sets the line.**                   | "You decide what it does on its own and what it brings to you first. It can never give itself more authority than you've granted." |
| 4   | **Everything is on the record.**                | "Every action it takes is written down in a record it cannot change, and you can read it any time."                                |
| 5   | **We run it.**                                  | "You're not buying something to figure out on your own. We set it up with you and we run it."                                      |

## Who it's for

| **First market**  | Small law firms, solo to 20 attorneys (ADR 0040), starting with plaintiff personal-injury firms.                                                                                  |
| **Why law first** | The coordinator and paralegal role is expensive and hard to keep filled, the work is document-heavy, and firms are used to paying for staff.                                      |

| **Beyond law**    | The same worker fits any business with a coordinator-shaped gap. Industry starting points are called packs. Keep this out of a law firm's room: it dilutes "built for your firm." |

## What a law firm actually gets

The judgment stays with the attorneys. The Operator does paralegal and coordinator work:

- **Medical chronologies:** reads a client's medical records into a dated, cited chronology. 18 delivered for our first client, from 29,000+ pages, every entry cited to the page it came from.
- **First drafts:** demand letters, case evaluation memos, mediation briefs, discovery responses, for an attorney to judge and finish.
- **File upkeep:** reviews the caseload, writes a current status into each file, proposes changes for a person to approve.
- **Coordination:** files incoming mail and documents to the right matter, tracks open provider balances, answers status questions from staff.
- **Requests by email:** anyone the firm authorizes emails the Operator like a colleague and gets the work back.

## What it is not

| It is not                              | Because                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Software the firm runs itself          | We run it. Adoption is our problem, not theirs.                                                                                                     |
| A chatbot or a phone answering service | It does the work inside the firm's systems, not just the first conversation.                                                                        |
| A lawyer                               | It never gives legal advice or makes a legal judgment. Say "paralegal and coordinator work." Never say "we don't do legal work"; that's inaccurate. |
| One fixed tool                         | Each firm's Operator is configured for that firm: its skills, its limits, its voice, its connections, its memory.                                   |

## The firm around it

Sometimes the right answer is an Operator. Sometimes it's a cleaner process or one integration, and we say so. That honesty is part of the pitch.
