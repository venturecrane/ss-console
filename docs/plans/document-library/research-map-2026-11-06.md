# Firm documents, style, attorney and audience: what exists today

Research map, 2026-11-06. Step 1 of the document-library work. No design here.

**The promise being checked** (ADR 0083, `docs/adr/0083-authorship-model-output-classes.md`, with its 2026-08-19 amendment at line 176, and `docs/handbook/operator-platform.md:105-107`): a firm's documents come out in the firm's own Word files, in the firm's style or the named attorney's, written for the document's reader, and a correction made once holds.

**Verdict in one line.** The promise is met for the firm's fill-in forms and, in format only, for demand letters. For every other document it is not met, because five separately built mechanisms each hold a piece and the paths that produce documents on a client seat read almost none of them.

## How this was made, and its limits

- Four read-only traces of the code on `origin/main` at `3405d72e5`, one per mechanism, each cell carrying a file and line. I re-read the lines the conclusions rest on.
- The client seat's authored config in `venturecrane/engagements` at `origin/main` `6c06ffa` (`operator/customers/<client>/drafting/drafting-firm.yaml`, `demand/demand-firm.yaml`).
- Seat facts come from three sources, named where used: `operator/bin/fleet status` (run 11-06), the drafting session's seat reads of 11-06, and the de-identified drafting answer key (`docs/plans/logic-in-code/drafting-answer-key-2026-11-05.md`, "KEY" below).
- Tests: the drafting lane's files ran offline (1,265 passed, 11 skipped); 16 overlay spec files (314 passed) and 5 `operator/bin` spec files (64 passed). The connector and demand tests were not run locally; CI runs them (`.github/workflows/operator-substrate.yml:252,332`).
- Not established: whether the firm's attorneys differ in how they write, as opposed to how their documents look (a read-only measurement is running in the client ops workspace); and anything on the pilot seat, which had no read route.

Built, wired and tested are used as `CLAUDE.md` defines them: built = code and its tests exist; wired = every gate between a real request and the effect is open on a seat a client uses; tested = someone observed it on a real seat.

## 1. What "the document library" is, from the record

The firm's own folder of Word files in its case system: one template per document class, plus the firm's own forms and reference templates. Seven classes: `discovery_set`, `discovery_response`, `demand_letter`, `mediation_brief`, `memo`, `depo_outline`, `letter` (`operator/connectors/smokeball/smokeball_connector/docx_format.py:81-89`, file names at `library.py:48-56`). The Operator establishes it on an administrator's word from the firm's own exemplars (`operator/skills/document-library-establishment/SKILL.md`). The stated property: "Typography is not config: the firm edits a style in Word and the next draft follows, with no publish and no reboot" (`operator-platform.md:107`).

## 6. Where the record differs from the working assumptions

1. **"One style, no audience" is true of the drafting lane, not of demands.** The demand lane already picks an attorney's own format in code from the matter's responsible attorney, and already carries an adjuster profile. That is the reusable shape; it stops at layout and at one reader.
2. **The record names more readers than the ADR's four.** Client, adjuster or carrier, opposing counsel, court (seat list and ADR); internal (ADR and the 09-08 profiles); the mediator (the brief's source check reads "as opposing counsel, then as the mediator", `drafting/verify/prompts.py:20`); and vendors and providers (`outbound_vendor`, `operator/contracts/output-classes.yaml`). The client seat's authored list omits internal; the profiles produced for it were adjuster, court and internal.
3. **An attorney's documents are not always written by that attorney.** The firm's demands are typed by one staff member for every attorney and signed by the responsible attorney (`demand-firm.yaml` provenance notes). "The attorney whose name goes on it" selects the right format; whether it selects a distinct way of writing is unmeasured.
4. **Per-attorney wording has no evidence yet either way.** The only study rated an author split "insufficient evidence", on a synthetic corpus (`docs/research/voice-distillation/2026-08-01-spec-representation-bakeoff.md` section 2.2), and the pilot's spec says register follows audience, not author. On 2026-09-09 the ruling of record was that no attorney gets a personal variant. The Captain's words of 11-06 (the two attorneys already drafted for, "but really all of them") replace that. What the firm's own files show per attorney and class is a count to take at no cost before design is fixed.
5. **Establishing from accepted documents is how the second attorney's demand format was made**, by hand, from one signed demand the firm sent. Nothing automates it.
6. **Even where the library is read, the Word-edit promise is narrower than the handbook says.** Inline formatting, class layout, the signature style and all template text do not follow an edit.
7. **The portal shows a voice file's body only**, not corrections or who made them (`src/components/portal/operator/OutputClassSpecs.astro:107`); `docs/handbook/operator-console.md:139` says otherwise.
8. **Every acceptance box in ADR 0083 and ADR 0085 is unticked.** Two are contradicted by the tree: the retired mechanism is not removed, and the gate does not check conformance.
