A public figure with no row here is not allowed: `tests/landing-page.test.ts` checks that every proof-tile value on the home page and the case study appears below in backticks.

**Rules.**

- Figures describe our own delivered work, stated as a floor ("29,000+"), never a projection.
- No dollar amounts, no client identity, nothing read out of a client's files (the client's confidentiality terms bar marketing use of it). Sources that live in the private engagements repo are cited by document, never quoted.
- When a figure moves, update the row and the page in the same PR.
