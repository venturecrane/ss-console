## One-liners

**The firm.** We build and run AI workers that take on the work falling between your people and your software.

**For a law firm.** An Operator that carries a personal injury case from the records to the settlement, built and run for you.

## Thirty seconds

SMD Services is a software and AI solutions firm. Our flagship is the Operator: a worker we build for your business and run for you. It works inside the systems you already use, does only what you have authorized, and keeps a record of everything it does. For a plaintiff personal injury firm, that means reading medical records into cited chronologies whenever the firm asks. We have delivered 18 of them, from 29,000+ pages of records, at a small fraction of what outsourced chronology services charge per page. It starts with an assessment.

## Two minutes

Every business has a layer of work that falls between its people and its software: the handoff, the follow-up, the reading nobody has time for. Usually a person holds it together by hand.

We are a software and AI solutions firm, and our flagship, the Operator, is a new kind of worker for that layer. We build it around how you actually run, connect it to the systems you already pay for, and run it for you. You decide what it does on its own and what waits for a person, and it can never widen that line itself. Every action it takes is on the record, and the record is tamper-evident.

Here is what that looks like in practice. A plaintiff personal injury firm lives on medical records. Our Operator reads them into cited chronologies when the firm asks: 18 delivered so far, from 29,000+ pages, every entry cited to its page, at a small fraction of what outsourced services charge per page. The firm kept asking for more: first drafts of a demand letter, a case evaluation memo, and a mediation brief, each for an attorney to judge and finish. It also keeps a working list of settled cases and the provider balances still open. The judgment stays with the attorneys.

And we are honest about fit. Not every problem needs an Operator. Sometimes the answer is a cleaner process or one integration. The assessment is how we find out.
