Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) capturing strategic and technical decisions made during the SMD Services platform build.

> **Looking for the substantive decision corpus?** The numbered go-to-market decisions (34 active across buy box, scope, pricing, assessment, distribution, delivery; numbered through #51; 3 superseded: #2, #12, #43) live in [decision-stack.md](./decision-stack.md). The numbered ADRs below capture narrower architectural choices.

> **Numbering note:** the sequence is not dense — **0013 and 0014 were never assigned** (the index jumps 0012 → 0015; no files were lost). ADR 0044 briefly collided with a second record; the proposed static-secret connector contract was renumbered to [0061](./0061-static-secret-connector-contract.md) on 2026-07-03, and [0044-r2-authoritative-live-reconfig.md](./0044-r2-authoritative-live-reconfig.md) is the canonical 0044. ADR 0068 similarly collided on 2026-07-07 (two records drafted in parallel); [0068-portal-ia-offerings-as-destinations.md](./0068-portal-ia-offerings-as-destinations.md) is the canonical 0068 (merged first), and the independent-oversight-plane record was renumbered to 0069. That renumber then produced a **third** collision: 0069 was already taken by the operator-legibility record (also 2026-07-07). Resolved 2026-07-13 — [0069-operator-legibility-progressive-configurability.md](./0069-operator-legibility-progressive-configurability.md) keeps 0069 (it is the record wired into shipped code and cited by number), and the independent-oversight-plane record was renumbered to [0074](./0074-independent-oversight-plane.md) (0073 was taken in the interim by [0073-remove-law-external-send-floor.md](./0073-remove-law-external-send-floor.md), so oversight moved to the next free slot). A CI guard (`tests/adr-integrity.test.ts`) now blocks any duplicate numeric prefix and any file missing from this index.
